## Findings

The policy permits dissemination of aggregate statistics that do not correspond to, or allow
derivation of, participant counts of 1–20 (see V-03). It does not discuss models fitted to such
aggregates.

## Decision

Nothing in Milestones 1–4 depends on this. Demo models are trained on independently generated
synthetic data only (FR-097, FR-098). No summary-statistics-to-model pathway is built.

## Remaining uncertainty

Whether such models may leave the Workbench (affects a possible later demo strategy only).
