# UNF Artificial Intelligence Research Organization (AIRO)

The Artificial Intelligence Research Organization (AIRO) is a student organization at the University of North Florida focused on learning about, developing, and applying artificial intelligence and machine learning technologies.

AIRO is open to students of all experience levels. Our meetings combine introductions to AI and machine learning concepts with hands-on coding examples and collaborative projects.

## Meetings

AIRO meets Thursdays at 6:00 p.m. at the UNF Library.

Meeting materials, presentations, and coding examples are available in the [`meetings`](./meetings) folder.

## Projects

AIRO members work together on semester-long artificial intelligence and machine learning projects.

Project code and documentation are available in the [`projects`](./projects) folder.

## Resources

Datasets, tutorials, references, and other useful AI and machine learning resources are available in the [`resources`](./resources) folder.
